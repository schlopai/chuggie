# Art sources

The images in this directory are GENERATED by `scripts/gen_oakhollow.py` from the packs below. The
script crops, recolours, quantises to the GBA's 15-colour sprite budget and packs; it does not copy
any source file wholesale. (The `.tmj` levels and `tiles.tsj` were seeded by the same script but are
now hand-maintained in Tiled — see the example's README.)

## DARK - Hero (Free Version) — the player

`hero.png` is **copied from [`examples/dark-hero`](../../dark-hero/assets/)**, which bakes it from
the "DARK - Hero" (Free Version) 48×48 platformer character pack; the two examples share one player
character. See that example's `ATTRIBUTION.md` for the pack and its terms.

> ⚠️ Same caveat as dark-hero: the pack shipped **without a bundled licence file**. Confirm the terms
> on its store page and record the author and licence before redistributing.

The townsfolk, their dialog portraits and the shop ware icons are NOT from this pack — they are still
recoloured rvros Adventurer sprites, generated by the script (see below).

## Sunny Land — Luis Zuno (Ansimuz)

**CC0 / public domain.** The pack's own licence PDF is vendored beside the `sunny-land` example at
[`examples/sunny-land/LICENSE-sunny-land.pdf`](../../sunny-land/assets/LICENSE-sunny-land.pdf).

Used for the whole world: the tileset (ground, masonry, planks, vines, props), the four building
facades (`house`, `wooden-house`, `straw-house`, `plant-house`), the door and signpost, the ladder
rungs (cropped out of the tree-house prop — the only ladder art in the pack), and the sky and
treeline the two parallax layers are built from.

→ `atlas.png`

## Adventurer 1.5 and Adventurer Hand Combat — rvros

**⚠️ No licence file ships in either pack.** The author's itch.io pages state the sprites are free to
use in commercial and non-commercial projects with credit and no resale, but that is not vendored
here as a text file, so **confirm the current terms before redistributing this example's art**. This
is the same situation as `examples/dark-hero`, which carries the same caveat.

Used for every character in the town: the player's fifteen animation clips (idle, walk, run, jump,
fall, land, crouch, crouch-walk, slide, ladder-climb, ledge grab, ledge climb, wall-slide, hurt,
death) and all eight townsfolk, which are the same sprite rotated around the colour wheel so a
recoloured villager keeps every shadow the artist drew.

→ `hero.png`, `npcs.png`, and the portraits at the front of `ui32.png`

## Ninja Adventure — Pixel-boy & AAA

**CC0.** Vendored in full at [`assets/ninja-adventure/`](../../../assets/ninja-adventure/) with its
own `LICENSE.txt`.

Used for the shop ware icons (potion, scroll, dish, boots, ring, amulet, kunai, shuriken, armour,
helm, guard, arrows), taken from the "Skill Icon" set.

→ the middle of `ui32.png`

## Drawn here

The selection cursor (a small yellow ►) is drawn by the generator — pixel fonts have no
right-pointing triangle glyph, so a menu cursor has to be a sprite.
